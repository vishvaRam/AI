LLM Sampling & Token Selection Sandbox

Simulating Temperature Scaling, Nucleus Slicing, and Repetition Logit Deflation

When an LLM generates text, it maps out a raw list of scores (Logits) for every word in its vocabulary. Sampling parameters shape this distribution before the final token choice is made. Adjust the sliders below to see how the candidate probability distribution modifies in real time.

Hyperparameter Panel

Temperature ($T$) 1.00

Scales logits. Low values collapse choice down to deterministic picks; high values flatten diversity.

Top-K Cutoff 5

Hard token limitation boundary. Discards all candidates outside the top $K$ choices.

Top-P (Nucleus) 1.00

Dynamic selection pool. Truncates candidates the instant their cumulative probability hits $P$.

Penalty Logit Adjusters
Historical Token Repetition Count 0

Simulates how many times the candidate token "learning" has appeared in past outputs.

Frequency Penalty 0.00

Deflates logits linearly based on *how many times* a word has been used.

Presence Penalty 0.00

Deflates logits by a flat amount if a word has appeared *at least once*, encouraging subject shifts.
