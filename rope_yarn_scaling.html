Why Vanilla RoPE Fails at Long Context

Rotary Positional Embeddings (RoPE) represent positions as complex rotational vectors. If an LLM pre-trained on 4K context receives a 32K token stream, its low-frequency dimensions rotate beyond recognized mathematical distributions, blinding the attention matrix. **YaRN (Yet Another RoPE extensioN)** solves this by partition-scaling waves dynamically depending on their target frequency channels.

High-Frequency Bands

Waves that govern precise, close-range local tokens relationships. YaRN isolates these channels and **leaves them completely un-interpolated**, ensuring the model preserves its exact near-neighbor semantic resolution.

Low-Frequency Bands

Waves that track macro-level, long-range global context patterns. YaRN applies a smooth **linear compression scale** to these tracks, pulling extreme out-of-bounds angles back down into known territories.

Entropy Scale Correction

Expanding context spreads attention probabilities over thousands of additional choices, diluting core values. YaRN calculates a specialized scale multiplier ($t$) to recalibrate attention weights, keeping response coherence stable.
