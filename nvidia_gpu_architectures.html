Numerical Precision Evolution in Hardware

As Large Language Models scale to hundreds of billions of parameters, hardware computation boundaries must adapt. Standard 32-bit floats provide extreme precision but clog memory lanes. Modern compute architectures focus on mixed-precision scaling—introducing specialized micro-formats like TF32, mixed FP8, and compressed FP4 to multiply deep-learning training and inference velocities while preserving reasoning boundaries.
