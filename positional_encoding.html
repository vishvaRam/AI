As you move the position slider (Step 1), notice how the Rotated Vector turns. The distance from the center (magnitude) is identical, but the angle *is* the position information. Modern models like Qwen 3.5 apply this math across all vector dimension pairs.

🧠 Why do Qwen and Gemma use RoPE?

🏷️

Vectors Don't Have Order

When an LLM first converts the words "fox" and "jumps" into vectors, those vectors only represent *meaning*, not location. Without positional encoding, the model reads sentences as unordered bags of words.

🔄

The Rotary Fix (RoPE)

Instead of *adding* numbers (older methods like Llama 1), RoPE (Rotary Positional Embedding) *multiplies* concept vectors by a rotation matrix. The angle of rotation is strictly tied to the token's index m in the sequence.

📏

Relative Distance

Crucially, RoPE allows the model's Self-Attention mechanism to naturally determine the *distance* between any two tokens (e.g., fox at pos 3 and jumps at pos 4) simply by looking at the angle difference between their rotated vectors. This handles long-range context exceptionally well.
